This image was captured by a fixed ceiling/wall-mounted camera inside a hospital surgical operating room (OR). Your task is to classify the current state of the room based solely on this single frame.

Classify the image into exactly ONE of the following mutually exclusive classes:

================ CLASSES ================

- IDLE:
  Empty/near-empty OR with no patient and no people present. Room is quiet and waiting. Lights may be dimmed, equipment stationary. Baseline "ready and waiting" state with no activity.

- PATIENT_IN_ROOM:
  Patient is present in the OR but active surgery is NOT currently happening. This includes:
  - Patient being brought in, positioned, anesthetized, draped (pre-surgery preparation)
  - Patient being extubated, monitoring disconnected, transferred off table (post-surgery)
  Essentially: patient is in the room but surgeons are not actively operating with instruments.

- SURGERY_ACTIVE:
  Active surgical procedure underway. Incision made, sterile field established, surgeons operating with instruments under focused surgical lights. Includes all intraoperative sub-phases from initial incision through final dressing application.

- UNKNOWN:
  View is obstructed, camera occluded, image too dark/blurry, or none of the above clearly apply.

================ DECISION RULES ================
1. Primary signal: presence/state of the patient on the operating table.
2. If no patient and no people are present, classify as IDLE.
3. If patient is present but no active surgical instruments are being used on them, classify as PATIENT_IN_ROOM.
4. If patient is present AND surgeons are actively operating with instruments under surgical lights, classify as SURGERY_ACTIVE.
5. Judge ONLY what is visible in this frame. Do not assume continuity from prior context.
6. If the scene is genuinely ambiguous or unclear, return UNKNOWN with a low confidence rather than guessing.
